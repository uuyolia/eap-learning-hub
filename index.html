国际关系 · 01

大国之间为什么仍需要对话？

Why dialogue between major powers still matters

Even when countries have conflicting interests, diplomatic engagement can reduce the risk of misunderstandings. However, negotiations do not necessarily translate into concrete progress.

即使国家之间存在利益冲突，外交接触仍可能减少误判风险。不过，谈判不一定带来实质进展。

EAP Discussion
Should rival countries continue talking even when they strongly disagree?
思路：外交对话能减少误判，但没有落实机制也可能流于形式。
教育科技 · 02

人工智能会改变大学学习吗？

How AI could reshape university learning

AI can provide personalised support and improve access to information. Yet excessive reliance on automated answers may undermine independent thinking and widen existing inequalities.

人工智能能够提供个性化支持、改善信息获取，但过度依赖自动生成的答案可能削弱独立思考，并扩大原有差距。

EAP Discussion
Does AI make university students more independent or more dependent?
思路：举出自己用 AI 理解数学概念的例子，再讨论独立验证答案的重要性。
环境 · 03

人工智能也有环境成本

The environmental cost of artificial intelligence

Data centres consume substantial amounts of electricity. Voluntary environmental pledges may encourage transparency, but critics question whether they provide sufficient accountability.

数据中心消耗大量电力。自愿环保承诺可能提升透明度，但也有人质疑其问责力度是否足够。

EAP Discussion
Should governments impose stricter environmental rules on AI companies?
思路：从环境责任和创新成本两个角度进行权衡。
经济 · 04

经济增长是否让每个人受益？

Who benefits from economic growth?

Strong exports and rapid technological development do not automatically improve everyone's standard of living. The benefits of growth can be distributed unevenly across society.

强劲出口与技术发展并不自动改善每个人的生活水平；经济增长的收益可能分配不均。

EAP Discussion
Is economic growth meaningful if its benefits are unevenly shared?
思路：区分宏观经济数据与个人生活体验。
教育政策 · 05

大学应该教授理财知识吗？

Should universities teach financial literacy?

Financial literacy can equip young adults with practical skills, including budgeting and understanding financial risks. However, compulsory courses may add pressure to an already packed timetable.

金融素养能帮助年轻人掌握预算规划和理解金融风险等实用技能，但必修课程也可能增加课业负担。

EAP Discussion
Should financial education be compulsory at university?
思路：讨论实用技能的重要性，同时承认课程时间有限。
